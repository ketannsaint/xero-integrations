First-version boundary

Designed to be useful without becoming inventory software.

Included in the concept

  • Authorised supplier bills in the organisation's base currency
  • Items already present in native Xero Products and Services
  • Separate approval for purchase and optional sale defaults
  • Source-linked receipt, verification and guarded rollback

Not included

  • Foreign currency, landed costs, packs or unit conversions
  • Repricing existing invoices, bills or purchase orders
  • Changing tracked-inventory valuation or average cost
  • Automatic unattended writes or a replacement inventory system
